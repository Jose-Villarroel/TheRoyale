# The Royale - general information for the Royale Concierge

## Location and contact
- Address: The Royale, Fifth Avenue, Midtown Manhattan, New York, NY, United States.
- Front desk and reservations: +1 507 3165 232 (24 hours, every day).
- The front desk can help with anything the concierge cannot answer.

## Check-in and check-out
- Check-in from 15:00. Check-out until 12:00 (noon).
- Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability; request them at the front desk.
- Luggage storage is complimentary before check-in and after check-out.
- A valid photo ID is required at check-in.

## Booking and cancellation
- Bookings are made online with the "Book this room" button or on the booking page, after signing in.
- A reservation can be cancelled from "My reservations" only before the arrival day; from the arrival day on it can no longer be cancelled.
- A reservation cannot be cancelled while its account has an outstanding balance.
- The concierge cannot create, modify or cancel bookings.

## Guests, children and pets
- Each room type has a maximum number of guests (see its description).
- Children are welcome. Cribs are available on request at no extra charge; they count toward the room's guest limit.
- Small pets (dogs and cats up to 10 kg) are welcome in Normal, Executive and VIP rooms with prior notice to the front desk; service animals are always welcome.

## Restaurants and opening hours
- Breakfast Lounge: daily 06:30 to 10:30 (until 11:00 on weekends).
- Signature Restaurant (contemporary New York cuisine): lunch 12:00 to 15:00, dinner 18:00 to 23:00.
- Craft Cocktail Bar: daily 17:00 to 01:00.
- 24h Room Service: the full a la carte menu delivered to the room at any time.
- Private Dining Room for up to 12 guests, by reservation through the concierge.
- Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and allergen-aware options are available; tell the staff about any allergy.

## Wellness
- Heated indoor pool: daily 06:00 to 22:00. Gym: open 24 hours.
- Spa treatments by appointment through the front desk or the concierge.

## Wi-Fi, parking and transport
- Premium Wi-Fi is complimentary in all rooms and public areas; the Business Center has dedicated fiber up to 1 Gbps.
- Valet parking is available 24 hours for a nightly fee; self-parking is not available at the hotel.
- Airport transfers from JFK, LaGuardia and Newark can be arranged through the Transportation service.

## Payments
- Room charges and services used during the stay are added to the room account and settled at the hotel.
