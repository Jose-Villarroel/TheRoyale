01 — The Service

An Hour Where Nothing Is Asked of You

The spa occupies the whole of the second floor: four treatment rooms, a sauna, a Turkish bath and a lap pool lit from above. Therapists are on staff, not contracted, and the same person will treat you throughout your stay if you prefer.

Treatments are booked in sixty or ninety minute blocks. Arrive twenty minutes early; the sauna and pool are yours before and after.
